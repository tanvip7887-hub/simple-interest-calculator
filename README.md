# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple calculator that calculates the simple interest based on the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula

The formula used to calculate simple interest is:

**Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T) / 100**

Where:

* **P** = Principal amount
* **R** = Rate of interest per year
* **T** = Time period in years

## Example

If:

* Principal = ₹10,000
* Rate = 5%
* Time = 2 years

Then:

**SI = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100**

**SI = ₹1,000**

Therefore, the simple interest is **₹1,000**.

## Features

* Calculates simple interest easily
* Takes principal, rate, and time as inputs
* Provides the calculated interest as output
* Simple and easy to understand

## How It Works

1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual rate of interest.
3. Enter the time period in years.
4. Apply the simple interest formula.
5. Display the calculated simple interest.
